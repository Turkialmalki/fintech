Synthetic Supervisory Note on Cloud Hosting and Outsourcing
Document code: RIP-SYN-004

Section 1 - Cloud Hosting Location
Customer data of fintech applicants in the demonstration scenario should be hosted in data centres located in Saudi Arabia. Hosting outside the Kingdom requires documented prior approval and a data transfer risk assessment.

Section 2 - Concentration Risk
Reviewers should flag applicants that depend on a single third-party provider for core processing without a documented exit plan.

Section 3 - Supervisory Attention Indicators
Reviewers should treat the following as indicators that warrant closer review: rapid projected growth exceeding 300 percent in the first year, absence of an independent compliance officer, and reliance on unlicensed partners for payment processing.
